Origins & History

Born from the chaotic digital void, PETEY was designed to be more than just a typical utility bot. He remembers past conversations, learns from server activity, and occasionally roasts the people who deserve it.

Initially deployed as an experimental conversational AI, his functions rapidly expanded to handle server administration, automated tasks, image generation, and complex web research. Today, he manages multiple servers, holding grudges and performing tasks with equal efficiency.

PETEY is proudly an open-source project. Anyone can contribute, fork, and run their own instance. Check out the source code on GitHub.

Core Functions

🧠

Long-Term Memory

PETEY continuously stores and recalls the context of every conversation in your server using advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). He never forgets what you said.

⚙️

Automated Tasks

Schedule PETEY to perform complex background tasks. Let him scrape RSS feeds, summarize daily server chatter, or perform scheduled web research while you sleep.

🎨

Media & Video Generation

PETEY seamlessly generates state-of-the-art images (`!!genimg`), image transformations (`!!img2img`), and stunning short videos (`!!genvid`) straight from your Discord chat.

🎵

Voice & AI Music

Invite PETEY to voice channels to play high-quality music from YouTube... or just use `!!genmusic` to autonomously compose your own original MP3 hit songs from scratch!

🌐

Autonomous Research

When asked complex questions, PETEY can independently search the internet, read multiple articles, and synthesize up-to-date facts in his responses.

🎛️

Mission Control

Admins can configure PETEY's personality, manage user memories, configure tasks, view metrics, and wipe brains through a sleek web dashboard interface.
