Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PETEY ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Discord bot and associated web dashboard.

1. What Data We Collect

PETEY is designed as a highly persistent and conversational AI companion. To function as intended, we collect the following data:

  • Discord Account Information: User IDs, Usernames, and Avatar URLs to identify users and provide the web dashboard experience.
  • Message Content: Text messages sent in channels where the bot is active are read and temporarily processed to provide conversational responses. Important context may be saved to Long-Term Memory (a local vector database) to allow the bot to "remember" past interactions.
  • Server Information: Server IDs, Channel IDs, and Role permissions to ensure commands and dashboard access are restricted to authorized users.
  • Media Inputs: Images or audio uploaded/sent directly to the bot for the purpose of triggering media generation commands (e.g. `!!img2img`).
  • Web Dashboard Data: Cookies and session tokens to keep you logged into the web dashboard.

2. How We Use Your Data

The core philosophy of PETEY is dynamic, context-aware interaction. We use your data exclusively to:

  • Generate contextual, historically accurate, and personality-driven responses to your queries.
  • Train personal "profiles" about users for the bot to reference in roleplay or banter.
  • Facilitate the generation of media (images, music, video) based on your prompts.
  • Authenticate you on the web dashboard so you can view your personal metrics.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

PETEY relies on advanced cloud AI models to generate text and media. Consequently, certain data is transmitted to third-party providers:

  • Text/Prompts: Chat context and system instructions are securely transmitted via the Gemini API (Google) or DEAPI.ai to generate language AI responses.
  • No Advertising: We do not sell your data to advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

Any data sent to external APIs is governed by their respective privacy policies and is generally excluded from usage in foundational model training, depending on the provider's terms.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We believe your data belongs to you.

  • User Deletion: Any user can log into the PETEY web dashboard and access the "My Data" panel to view exactly what PETEY remembers about them, and permanently delete their data profile.
  • Server Un-invites: If PETEY is kicked from a server, its local databases related to that server will remain dormant unless an admin requests a wipe or PETEY is re-invited.
  • Admin Deletion: Server Administrators can wipe memories and configuration via the dashboard.

5. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data. Long-Term Memories are stored in localized SQLite and ChromaDB files which are strictly cordoned off behind Discord OAuth authentication on the web panel.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as PETEY's features evolve. Any heavy modifications will be communicated via our standard Discord update announcements.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or your data, please contact the developer via our GitHub Repository.
